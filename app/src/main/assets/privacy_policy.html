Paper Leaf - PDF Reader

Privacy Policy | Effective Date: July 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Paper Leaf - PDF Reader mobile application created by the developer. This document explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use the Application.

Key Takeaway: Paper Leaf - PDF Reader is designed as an offline-first document viewer. Your PDF files, reading history, bookmarks, and preferences are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to any external server.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application does not require user registration or account creation. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, phone number, physical address, or contact list.

2. Device Permissions & File Access

To provide core functionality, the Application requires access to files on your device:

  • Storage & Storage/Media Access (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_DOCUMENTS): This permission is required solely to locate, open, display, and manage your local PDF documents within the app.
  • Notification Permission (Optional): Used locally on your device to remind you of your reading goals or habits if configured.

All document rendering and processing occurs entirely on your device. Your files are never transmitted off your device.

3. Third-Party Services

The Application may utilize standard third-party services provided by Google for app functionality and distribution (e.g., Google Play Services). These services may collect certain device telemetry information governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services Privacy Policy

4. Data Storage and Security

We prioritize the privacy and security of your data. Because all application data (including bookmarks, reading stats, and recent document lists) is stored locally using standard Android database storage (Room/SQLite), your data is as secure as your physical device.

5. Children’s Privacy

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Any updates will be effective immediately upon posting on this page.
